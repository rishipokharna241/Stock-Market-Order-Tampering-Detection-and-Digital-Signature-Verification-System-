Understanding the result

  • Valid: the order fields match the data covered by the signature.
  • Invalid: the signed fields changed, the signature is wrong, or the public key does not match.

Verification checks whether the signed fields match the saved record. It does not authenticate the person who created the key or protect records stored in this browser.
